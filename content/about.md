Gareth Wiecko is a Welsh-born pianist and producer currently living in Melbourne, Australia.
Having released his debut album [Notes to Self](https://open.spotify.com/album/3okqjG6JRNIaptv59rgNbj?si=lLRoTJPwTwuKmLXO8oiKxQ&nd=1) in 2011, he then went on to compose for an array
of art forms. Inspired by the fusion of his classical piano background and a passion for the
electronic scene, he strives to meld the two into his own unique style.

Having spent a few years traveling the globe, Gareth began studying composition and
production at AIM Melbourne, Australia from 2015-2017. Upon graduating, he began to
compose professionally for various bodies. In 2018 he composed a set of [introductions to
podcasts for Lipp Media](https://soundcloud.com/garethwiecko/podcast-introduction-lipp-media-1), a Melbourne based queer podcast channel. He was also approached
in the same year to compose the music for Grand Rapids Ballet in America, a 20 minute ballet
performance titled [They/Them](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1IBe7sZd4rE).

2019 saw Gareth collaborating with video game developer Thomas Janson. He composed the
music for [Death Hall](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iLLVMPc_29s), a side-scrolling platformer consisting of a giant monster chasing the main
character through the levels. During early 2020, Gareth was approached by Loughlan Prior to
compose the music for a contemporary dance performance titled [‘Scribble’](https://vimeo.com/469581246?embedded=true&source=vimeo_logo&owner=12357868), an interdisciplinary
dance film which merges the physicality of the human body with mesmerising animation. Gareth
was also approached in 2021 to compose the music for an introduction video for the property
company [BEKL](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1EyPFbqd45tGJ9Ls6tuUGV8RZYDs1_VEk/view?usp=sharing). Also during that year, City of Melbourne collaborated with Gareth to compose
music for the Melbourne Town Hall Organ.

During the last few years, Gareth has been working on his solo piano works, releasing a series
of ambient, minimal piano pieces. His single [‘INNI’](https://open.spotify.com/album/4bXF8NzFuHlX9ozlj7b0qP?si=njCsBnOuSLCMkCoLnbfMFg&nd=1), released in 2021 marked the beginning of a
new chapter. His second single [‘Pads’](https://open.spotify.com/album/6hAirMn60WsCt1LCNMisPA?si=7XNuHsTdQJOIRYle8znvxw&nd=1), released in 2022 was a stripped back solo piano piece,
returning to his roots. 2023 saw Gareth create his [‘IO’](https://open.spotify.com/track/31W5XNWK6emXTFHaszqxGn?si=a3c17fec50974bfb), a muted solo piano with birdsong as a
backing. His latest single [‘Eira’](https://open.spotify.com/album/5BTX9CGikZq7aSfobut8Gq?si=58hulz-mTrKhubw3JFWyLg) was a stripped-back piano track with warm pads as a backing.

He also finalised an album of desert themed tabletop music during this year. Titled [‘Sands of
Solaro’](https://open.spotify.com/album/6U6wLLRgPqVvUb0qqtLTJ3?si=deN585iqRmuRn3cbLSkgHA), it was a passion project of ten desert themed orchestral pieces to be used as an asset
pack, and also for tabletop gaming.

2023 to the current day have been busy years for Gareth, seeing him working on the
soundtracks for a selection of video games. [‘Gunboat God’](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2511210/Gunboat_God/) (to be released in 2026) is a digital
based score with over 30 energetic, pumping tracks. [‘Polar Pathways’](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2888890/Polar_Pathways/) due to be released in
2025 uses a minimal/ambient score with warm washed out melodies to complement the cute
aesthetic. [‘Esin Reborn’](https://twitter.com/EsinRebornDev) uses emotional live-orchestration to accompany the beautiful action
RPG qualities. [‘Valora the RTS’](https://open.spotify.com/album/17rsNBAindigz9xZXRMIlu?si=qFAGsbr0S_2GSD9avI18DQ) uses a mix of digital and live techniques to portray the
difference between the day and night cycles of the game.

Gareth is keen to continue his development as a composer and produce music for further
projects!
